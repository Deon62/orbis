Earn a share of the spread on every trader you bring in.

For as long as they keep trading. Tracked to the trade, paid every week.

What you earn

20%Starter1 to 9 active referrals
28%Growth10 to 49 active referrals
35%Top50 or more active referrals

The percentage is of the spread orbisflow earns on your referrals' real-money contracts. Your tier is set by how many referrals traded in the last 30 days, and the higher rate applies to everyone once you reach it.

When the money is ready

  1. Monday to Sunday

    Your referrals trade. Earnings build up through the week and show as Pending.

  2. Monday

    The week closes. Every contract has settled, and the total is checked.

  3. Thursday

    The week's earnings move to your real balance and show as Paid.

  4. Ready to withdraw

    From that moment, like any other balance, to your verified method.

What counts

  • A referral signs up with your link or code, and verifies their identity.
  • Only real-money contracts count. Demo trading earns nothing.
  • Earnings from your own accounts, or accounts you control, are removed.
  • To withdraw, your own account must be verified.
