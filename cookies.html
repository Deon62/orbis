Cookie policy

The small files orbisflow stores in your browser, what each kind is for, and how to control them.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

01 / The short version
  • Essential cookies keep you signed in and your account secure.
  • Preference cookies remember choices like your theme and sound settings.
  • Analytics cookies help us see which pages work and which do not.
  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser at any time.
02 / What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. We also use similar browser storage for the same purposes, and this policy covers both.

03 / The cookies we use

Essential

Needed for sign-in, security and placing contracts. The platform does not work without them.

Preferences

Remember your settings, such as light or dark theme, sound and your last chart timeframe.

Analytics

Tell us, in aggregate, which pages are used and where people get stuck, so we can improve them.

04 / What we do not do

We do not use cookies to sell your data or to show you third-party advertising.

05 / Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will stop you signing in.
