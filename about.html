Six inputs, not four hundred.

A trading platform that grew by subtraction.

Why we built it

Most trading platforms grow by addition. Every release adds another panel, another order type, another tab, until the screen that should hold one decision holds four hundred controls.

orbisflow went the other way. A binary contract needs six inputs: the market, the direction, the stake, the duration, the payout and your confirmation. We built the screen around those six and put everything else behind a menu.

How we make money

From the spread built into each contract's payout, and nothing else. We do not sell order flow, we do not sell your data, and we do not take the other side of your trade as a position we want to win.

Client money

Funds are held in segregated accounts, separate from the money that runs the company. Withdrawals go to a verified method in your own name.
