About orbisflow

Six inputs, not four hundred.

A trading platform that grew by subtraction. We build one screen for one decision, and keep everything else out of the way until you ask for it.

Why we built it

Most platforms grow by addition. We went the other way.

Every release on a typical platform adds another panel, another order type, another tab, until the screen that should hold one decision holds four hundred controls.

A binary contract needs six inputs. We built the screen around those six and put everything else behind a menu, so the thing in front of you is always the trade.

How we make money

From the spread, and nothing else.

Our revenue is the spread built into each contract's payout. We do not sell order flow, we do not sell your data, and we do not take the other side of your trade as a position we want to win.

It is a deliberately boring business model. The only way we grow is if you keep trading, and the only way you keep trading is if the platform treats you fairly.

1Source of revenue: the payout spread

Client money

Your funds are kept apart from ours.

Client funds are held in segregated accounts, separate from the money that runs the company. Withdrawals go to a verified method in your own name, never to a third party.

Trading carries risk, and a binary contract can lose its whole stake. Nothing here is advice, and the demo account exists so that the first lesson costs nothing.
