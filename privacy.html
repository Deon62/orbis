Privacy policy

What personal data orbisflow collects, why we need it, who sees it, and how you can control it.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

01 / The short version
  • We collect what we need to run your account, verify who you are and meet the law.
  • We do not sell your personal data, or share it for advertising.
  • Payment and verification providers see only what they need to do their job.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or ask us to delete it.
02 / What we collect

Details you give us

  • Name, email, phone number and country.
  • Identity documents and proof of address, as required by law.
  • Payment method details, held by our payment providers.

Data from your use of orbisflow

  • Device, browser, IP address and approximate location.
  • The pages you visit, the contracts you place and your account settings.
03 / Why we collect it
  • To open and run your account, settle contracts and process payments.
  • To verify your identity and meet anti-money-laundering obligations.
  • To detect fraud, abuse and security threats.
  • To improve the platform and fix problems.
  • To send you service messages, and marketing only if you opt in.
04 / Who we share it with

We share data only with:

  • payment providers such as Paystack, to process deposits and withdrawals;
  • identity verification providers, to check your documents;
  • regulators, courts and law enforcement, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

05 / How long we keep it

We keep account and transaction records for as long as your account is open, and afterwards for as long as the law requires us to keep them for anti-money-laundering and tax purposes.

06 / How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to staff who need it, and two-step verification is available on every account.

07 / Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act of Kenya, and similar laws where you live, you can:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete data we are not required to keep;
  • withdraw consent to marketing at any time.

Contact us using the details below to use any of these rights.

08 / Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in and to understand how the platform is used. See our Cookie policy for details.

09 / Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will tell you by email before it takes effect.
