What we collect

Account details you give us: name, email, phone, country, and the identity documents required by law.

Usage data: device, browser, IP address, and the pages and contracts you interact with.

Why we collect it

To operate your account, settle contracts, process payments, and meet our regulatory obligations.

To detect fraud and abuse, and to improve the platform.

Who we share it with

Payment providers, identity verification providers, and regulators where required by law.

We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it.

Contact us to exercise any of these.
