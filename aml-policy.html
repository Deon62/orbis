AML policy

How orbisflow prevents money laundering and the financing of terrorism, and what that means for your account.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

01 / The short version
  • We verify the identity of every client before a withdrawal.
  • Money goes out only to a verified method in the account holder's own name.
  • We monitor transactions and report suspicious activity as the law requires.
  • We may pause an account or payment while we carry out checks.
02 / Our commitment

orbisflow Markets Ltd does not accept funds that come from crime, and does not let the platform be used to move them. This policy follows the Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act of Kenya and applicable international standards.

03 / Know your customer

Identity

We verify every client with a government ID and proof of address before a first withdrawal.

Enhanced checks

Where activity or amounts call for it, we may ask for more, such as proof of the source of funds.

04 / Payments
  • Deposits and withdrawals must use a method in the account holder's own name.
  • Third-party payments are refused and returned.
  • Funds are withdrawn to the method they came from wherever possible.
05 / Monitoring & reporting

We monitor accounts for unusual patterns, such as deposits followed by withdrawals with little or no trading. Where we suspect money laundering, we report it to the relevant authority and may be unable to tell you that we have done so.

06 / Restricted countries

We do not open accounts for residents of countries under international sanctions, or where our services are not permitted.

07 / Record keeping

We keep identity and transaction records for at least as long as the law requires after an account is closed.
