Risk disclosure

Binary options carry a high risk of losing money quickly. Please read this before you trade with real funds.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

01 / The short version
  • Every contract risks its full stake. If your prediction is wrong at expiry, you lose it.
  • Signals, AI insights and past results do not predict future outcomes.
  • Nothing on orbisflow is investment advice.
  • Only trade money you can afford to lose entirely, and start on demo.
02 / Capital at risk

Binary options are complex instruments. Each contract has two outcomes: you receive the stated payout, or you lose your stake. Losses can add up quickly over a series of contracts.

03 / No guaranteed outcome

Past performance, trading signals and AI output do not predict future results. Our signals are wrong a meaningful share of the time, and no strategy, course or tool removes the risk of loss.

04 / Market & technical risk
  • Prices can move sharply around news and economic releases, and spreads can widen.
  • Weekend and off-hours markets can behave differently from normal sessions.
  • Connection problems on your side or ours can affect when a contract is placed.
05 / Not advice

orbisflow does not give investment, financial or tax advice. Academy courses and market news are for education only. Decide for yourself, or speak to an independent adviser.

06 / Suitability

Before trading with real money, make sure you understand how these products work and whether they suit your finances. If you are unsure, keep using the demo account.

07 / Tools that help
  • Deposit caps, loss limits and cooling-off periods, under Preferences.
  • Self-exclusion, which pauses your account if you need to step away.
  • A free demo account with $10,000 in demo funds.
